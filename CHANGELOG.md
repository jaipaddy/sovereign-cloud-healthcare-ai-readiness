### Changed
- **Datadog for Government**: status updated from *FedRAMP High In Process (May 2025)* to **FedRAMP High Authorized as of May 6, 2026**. Source URL refreshed.

- New source links: Datadog FedRAMP High certification (May 2026), Section 1557 partial non-enforcement notice (May 2025), FedRAMP 20x Phase 2/3 timeline.
